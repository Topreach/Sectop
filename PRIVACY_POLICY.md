# Privacy Policy — Sectop

**Last Updated:** March 2025

## 1. Introduction

Sectop ("we", "our", "us") is a community safety and emergency response platform designed for Nigeria. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("Sectop") and related services.

By using Sectop, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

- **Account Information**: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a salted hash).
- **Profile Information**: Optional profile details such as your photo, location area, and responder status.
- **Incident Reports**: When you report an incident, we collect the details you provide, including descriptions, photos, audio recordings, and location data.
- **Tip-Offs**: Anonymous security tips you submit. These are stored without linking to your account identity.
- **Communications**: Messages sent through the app's chat and broadcast features.

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

- **Location Data**: We collect precise GPS location data when you:
  - Activate an SOS alert
  - Report an incident
  - Use location tracking features
  - Use the app in the background during an active SOS session
- **Device Information**: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and app version.
- **Usage Data**: Features used, interactions within the app, and crash reports.
- **Bluetooth Data**: For mesh network functionality, we collect nearby Bluetooth device identifiers to establish peer-to-peer connections.

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties

- **Authentication Services**: If you use third-party authentication, we receive information from the authentication provider as permitted by your privacy settings.
- **Map Services**: We use map services to display danger zones and route information. These services may collect location data according to their own privacy policies.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- **Emergency Response**: To process and respond to SOS alerts and emergency requests.
- **Safety Analytics**: To analyze incident patterns, predict danger zones, and recommend safe routes.
- **Distress Detection**: To analyze audio recordings for signs of distress using AI (in English, Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa, and Pidgin).
- **Community Safety**: To display incident reports and danger zones to other users for collective awareness.
- **Service Improvement**: To improve app functionality, AI models, and user experience.
- **Notifications**: To send emergency alerts, safety updates, and critical service communications.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Compliance)

If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for collecting and using your information depends on the specific data and context:

- **Consent**: You have given clear consent for us to process your data for specific purposes.
- **Legitimate Interests**: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests (e.g., providing safety services, improving the app) and is not overridden by your rights.
- **Legal Obligation**: Processing is necessary to comply with the law.

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 5.1 With Your Consent

We share your information when you explicitly consent, such as when you:
- Submit an incident report that becomes visible to other users in your area
- Activate an SOS alert that is shared with responders
- Send messages through the platform

### 5.2 With Service Providers

We may share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:
- Cloud hosting providers (for data storage and processing)
- Push notification services (for sending alerts)
- Analytics providers (for app improvement)
- Map service providers (for location-based features)

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the services we request.

### 5.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal requests by public authorities (e.g., court orders, law enforcement).

### 5.4 Emergency Situations

We may disclose information to protect the safety of users, responders, or the public, or to prevent loss of life or serious injury.

### 5.5 Aggregated Data

We may share aggregated, anonymized data that cannot reasonably identify you for research, safety analysis, or public reporting purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with services. Specifically:

- **Account Data**: Retained until account deletion.
- **Incident Reports**: Retained for safety analysis and legal compliance. You may request removal.
- **SOS Alerts**: Retained for emergency response records and safety analysis.
- **Audio Recordings**: Retained only as long as necessary for distress analysis and evidence purposes.
- **Location Data**: Retained for active sessions and deleted after the session ends, except when part of an incident report or SOS alert.
- **Anonymous Tip-Offs**: Retained for investigation purposes but cannot be linked to your identity.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us or using the account deletion feature in the app.

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your data:

- **Encryption at Rest**: All data stored on servers is encrypted using industry-standard encryption.
- **Encryption in Transit**: All data transmitted between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS/SSL.
- **Hardware-Backed Security**: On supported devices, cryptographic keys are stored in the Android KeyStore, a hardware-backed secure environment.
- **Biometric Authentication**: Optional biometric authentication for app access.
- **Access Controls**: Strict access controls limit which personnel can access user data.
- **Regular Audits**: We regularly review our security practices.

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

### 8.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your account information through the app settings.

### 8.2 Location Controls

You can control location sharing through your device settings. However, disabling location may limit core functionality such as SOS alerts and danger zone features.

### 8.3 Notification Preferences

You can manage notification preferences in the app settings and device settings.

### 8.4 Account Deletion

You can request account deletion through the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account). Upon deletion, your personal data will be permanently removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

### 8.5 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your data by contacting us.

### 8.6 Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

### 8.7 Additional Rights (EEA Users)

If you are in the EEA, you also have the right to:
- **Object** to processing of your personal data
- **Restrict** processing of your personal data
- **Lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority

## 9. Children's Privacy

Sectop is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, we will delete it immediately.

## 10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. For significant changes, we may provide additional notice through the app.

## 12. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

- **Email**: privacy@sectop.app
- **In-App**: Settings → Contact Support
